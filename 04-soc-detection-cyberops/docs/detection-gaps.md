## Infrastructure Limitations

- Security devices detect patterns, not intent.
- Encrypted traffic limits deep inspection.
- Evasion techniques can suppress alerts.
- Signature-based detection lags novel attacks.

## Inherent Limitations

### Intelligence and Encryption Constraints
- Threat intelligence may be outdated or incomplete
- Encrypted traffic obscures payload-level visibility
- Detection must rely on metadata and behavior

### Vulnerability and Data Interpretation Limits
- Vulnerabilities indicate risk, not compromise
- Logs reflect activity, not success
- Security data lacks business context

SOC decisions require correlation and analyst judgment.

### Endpoint Coverage Limitations
- Not all assets are monitored equally
- Endpoint agents can be disabled or bypassed
